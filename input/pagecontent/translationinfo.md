This guide is maintained in **German** and **English**. The pages under `/de/`
are the German edition; `/en/` is the English edition and also the default
rendering. Use the language switcher at the top right to move between them.

All narrative pages, the artifact introductions and the titles, descriptions
and element texts of profiles, extensions, code systems and value sets are
available in both languages. Code displays of external terminologies (SNOMED
CT, LOINC, CLO) are shown as published by their maintainers.
